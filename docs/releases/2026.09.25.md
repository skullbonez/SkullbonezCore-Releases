# SkullbonezCore 2026.09.25 — spline Director and crash-test ragdolls

**The Camera Director controls have changed. Camera A/B placement and A-to-B transitions have been removed. Director now moves one camera along a captured path.**

## Download and run

Extract the whole **SkullbonezCore-2026.09.25-win-x64.zip** into a writable folder and run **SKULLBONEZ_CORE.exe** (or **Launch SkullbonezCore.cmd**). Keep **SkullbonezData** and the DLLs beside it. Requires 64-bit Windows and a DirectX 12-compatible GPU and driver. This portable build is unsigned.

Click **Tutorial** for a guided introduction, or open the included first-time user manual. Existing scenes such as **marble_run.scene.json** and **solar_system.scene.json** remain available through **Tools > Scene**.

## Set up the new Director camera

1. Open a scene and enable **Edit scene** in **Editor > Controls**. Turn off **Minecraft mode**, if active, so its movement controls do not interfere with camera flight.
2. Open **Camera**, beside **Replay** in the lower-left panel. This tab is shown only while Edit mode is enabled.
3. Hold **right mouse** over the viewport to look around and use **W/A/S/D** to fly; **Q/E** moves down/up. Release right mouse, then click **Capture view**. Move to another viewpoint and capture again. The path supports 2–32 viewpoints; **Remove last** removes the most recent one.
4. Use **Curve** to choose **Spline** or **Linear** segments. Choose **Linear**, **Ease in**, **Ease out**, or **Ease in-out**. Easing applies once across the entire path, not separately at each viewpoint.
5. **Faster / Slower** adjusts the duration shown for the whole path. **Play path** enters Director mode. **Space** pauses/resumes; **Restart path** starts again from the first viewpoint. The camera stops at the last viewpoint.
6. Hold **B** to temporarily grab the camera for free flight. Release it to blend back to the path. Choose another camera mode in the top-bar dropdown to leave Director.
7. Click **Save path** to keep your work. **Load path** restores it from the scene's `.shot.json` sidecar, including curve, easing and duration. Keep this file with the scene when moving it. **Save scene** and **Save recording** are separate operations.

The old Camera A/B palette entries and saved A/B placements are no longer used. Recreate those shots with **Capture view**. Existing legacy shot-list files remain readable.

## Minecraft mouse controls changed

Enable **Edit scene**, then **Minecraft mode** in **Editor > Controls**. Click the viewport once to capture the mouse; this first click does not edit the scene. **Left-click now places** the selected object at the preview, and **right-click removes** a targeted supported standalone object. Press **Esc** to release the cursor. **F** switches flying/walking; **W/A/S/D** moves, **Space** rises or jumps, and **Shift** descends while flying. Turn off Minecraft mode to return to ordinary editor camera controls.

## Crash-test ragdolls

Ragdolls now have coordinated yellow/orange shells, charcoal joints, compact mittens and boots, a visor, chest number and Skullbonez emblem. Shared meshes and instance materials batch compatible parts across characters.

New ragdolls have **sphere head colliders with matching rotational inertia**. Their head mass, joints and joint settings are unchanged. Other body colliders retain their existing shapes. Older saved scenes with box head colliders retain those saved physics values and use rounded box head meshes. Cosmetic details add no simulated bodies or joints.

### See the actual collision shapes

1. Open **Physics**, then **Visualisation**.
2. Click **Ragdoll colliders** to cycle **Off**, **Selected Ragdolls**, and **All Ragdolls**. It defaults to **Off**.
3. In Edit mode, select any ragdoll part to show the complete selected ragdoll. **All Ragdolls** displays every ragdoll's shapes. Cyan lines remain visible over the solid meshes and follow the physics poses during playback and replay.
4. Cycle back to **Off** to hide the overlay. This is a viewing control, not a change to collision behaviour.

Scene save/reload preserves ragdoll physics and appearance. Replay remains separate from camera path authoring. In the three-ragdoll check, solids used five mesh draw calls; collision wireframes added one batched draw call for either Selected or All.

## Included and limitations

The earlier marble-run objects, group selection, fullscreen/windowed control and tutorial remain included. This update focuses on camera authoring and ragdoll presentation; it does not include the planned broader UI optimization work.

Save important scene edits, camera paths and recordings separately before closing or changing scenes. Sphere heads intentionally change new ragdolls' head contact and rotation behaviour compared with old box heads.

Solver Lab's optional comparison recordings are not included in this download.
